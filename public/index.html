Our story

Cooked with care, served with heart

Hearth & Table started in a home kitchen, cooking for friends' birthdays and backyard weddings. Word spread, and today we cater events of every size without losing that home-cooked feeling.

We shop local, cook everything from scratch and build each menu around the people you're feeding. From the first tasting to the final clean-up, you'll deal with the same small team that cooks your food.

  • Locally sourced, seasonal ingredients
  • Vegetarian, vegan and allergy-friendly options
  • Drop-off, buffet or fully staffed service
