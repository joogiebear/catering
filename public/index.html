IVOur Story

From a home kitchen to your table

Hearth & Table began the way the best things do: cooking for friends. A birthday here, a backyard wedding there, until word spread and the requests never stopped.

Today we cater events of every size without losing that home-cooked feeling. The same small team plans your menu, cooks your food and pours the last coffee.

— The Hearth & Table family
